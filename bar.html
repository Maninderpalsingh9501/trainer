Restaurant Automation System

Ministry of Bar Exchange Chandigarh.

Tech Stack: React-Native, Python, Microservices, Scikit-learn

Designed and implemented an automated ordering system for restaurants,allowing customers to place orders with a tap. Stored order records on a central server and facilitated automatic transmission of orders to the kitchen.
Total order report, automatic billing and Recommendation system is included in the application made for both android and ios.
Use Custom Machine Learning Model to predict the dynamic price based upon interest of people.
Currently deployed at the Ministry of Bar Exchange,Chandigarh, aiming to streamline restaurant operations and enhance customer experience.
